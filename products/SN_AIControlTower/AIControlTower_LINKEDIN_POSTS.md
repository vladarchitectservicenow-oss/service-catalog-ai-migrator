## Post 2: The Solution (Product)

---

**AIControlTower — centralized AI observability for your entire ServiceNow AI estate.**

One dashboard. All instances. All AI products. Real-time.

Here's how it works:

1️⃣ **Collector** — a lightweight scoped app on each instance reads Now Assist, Build Agent, and AI Agent tables every 15-60 minutes

2️⃣ **Hub** — telemetry streams via REST API to a central reporting instance that aggregates, deduplicates, and normalizes

3️⃣ **Dashboard** — one workspace shows your entire AI estate: interactions, users, success rates, failures, spend — across all instances and all products

4️⃣ **Governance** — automated alerts detect:
   • Success rate dropped >10% below 30-day average
   • Department adoption below 15%
   • Failure spike 2x above normal
   • <5% of users generating >65% of usage (concentration risk)
   • Instance hasn't synced in >24h

5️⃣ **ROI** — maps AI interactions to hours saved and dollars saved, with annualized benefit projection

The data model is extensible: Product → Capability → Interaction → Execution → Outcome. When ServiceNow launches a new AI product, you add a connector — no schema changes.
